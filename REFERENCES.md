## D. Physiology and toxicology

13. Morgan ET. Regulation of drug-metabolizing enzymes and transporters in
    infection, inflammation, and cancer. *Drug Metab Dispos*.
    2008;36(2):205–216. doi:10.1124/dmd.107.018747 — **verified** —
    inflammation-mediated down-regulation of CYP enzymes. *Note: this is in
    Drug Metabolism and Disposition, not Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics —
    a plausible misattribution a memory-written citation would likely make.*
14. Crane RJ, Jones KDJ, Berkley JA. Environmental enteric dysfunction: an
    overview. *Food Nutr Bull*. 2015;36(1_suppl1):S76–S87.
    doi:10.1177/15648265150361S113 — **verified** for EED as intestinal
    inflammation, reduced absorptive capacity and reduced barrier function,
    diagnosed by dual-sugar absorption testing. **Does not** support the
    specific sub-claims of villous blunting, raised gastric pH or bile-salt
    depletion.
15. Park JD, Cherrington NJ, Klaassen CD. Intestinal absorption of cadmium is
    associated with divalent metal transporter 1 in rats. *Toxicol Sci*.
    2002;68(2):288–294. doi:10.1093/toxsci/68.2.288 — **verified** — iron
    deficiency raises duodenal DMT1 mRNA 15-fold and cadmium retention ~10-fold.
    **Limit:** rats, cadmium only.
16. Goldberg A, Meredith PA, Miller S, Moore MR, Thompson GG. Hepatic drug
    metabolism and haem biosynthesis in lead-poisoned rats. *Br J Pharmacol*.
    1978;62(4):529–536. doi:10.1111/j.1476-5381.1978.tb07757.x — **verified** —
    lead depresses hepatic cytochrome P-450 and suppresses haem-synthesis
    enzymes; ALA synthase inversely correlates with microsomal P-450.
    **Limit:** rats.
17. Ruiz M. Into the labyrinth of the lipocalin α1-acid glycoprotein.
    *Front Physiol*. 2021;12:686251. doi:10.3389/fphys.2021.686251 —
    **partial** — confirms AGP is a positive acute-phase protein that binds
    mainly basic drugs. **Does not** state that the acute-phase rise lowers
    their free fraction; that step is an inference.
18. Roy NK, Murphy A, Costa M. Arsenic methyltransferase and methylation of
    inorganic arsenic. *Biomolecules*. 2020;10(9):1351.
    doi:10.3390/biom10091351 — **partial** — confirms SAM is the methyl donor.
    **Does not** support nutritional limitation of methylation capacity.
19. Patrick J, Reeds PJ, Jackson AA, Seakins A, Picou DIM. Total body water in
    malnutrition: the possible role of energy intake. *Br J Nutr*.
    1978;39(3):417–424. doi:10.1079/BJN19780057 — **verified** for elevated
    total body water in oedematous children, falling as oedema resolves
    (tritiated water, 65 children). Reports TBW only, **not** the
    extracellular/intracellular split.
20. Ikeda M, Zhang ZW, Moon CS, et al. Possible effects of environmental
    cadmium exposure on kidney function in the Japanese general population.
    *Int Arch Occup Environ Health*. 2000;73:15–25. doi:10.1007/PL00007933 —
    **partial** — supports cadmium-associated rises in urinary α1- and
    β2-microglobulin, i.e. tubular dysfunction, which is the pathway the
    simulator models. It did **not** measure GFR, and the authors call the
    evidence "of borderline significance". It therefore supports tubular injury
    but not a quantified reduction in filtration.

### The pattern, which matters more than the count

For five separate mechanisms the **general pharmacology is well sourced and
the nutrition-dependent limb is not**:

| Mechanism | General part | Nutrition-dependent part |
|---|---|---|
| Arsenic methylation | SAM is the methyl donor [18] | methyl-donor limitation under protein deficit — unsourced |
| DMT1 metal uptake | iron deficiency raises DMT1 and cadmium uptake in rats [15] | extension to lead, manganese, children — unsourced |
| AGP binding | positive acute-phase protein, binds basic drugs [17] | the resulting fall in free fraction — inference |
| Cadmium nephrotoxicity | tubular dysfunction markers rise with exposure [20] | reduced filtration — unsourced |
| Organ mass | organs are smaller in malnutrition | sub-proportional scaling, the 0.45 exponent — unsourced |

The coupling between nutritional status and toxicant handling — the project's
distinctive claim — therefore rests on mechanistic plausibility rather than on
measurement, in exactly the way the manuscript says the rest of the simulator
does. This is stated plainly in the limitations rather than left for a reader
to notice.
